# Gold intraday testing: source notes

Researched 2026-09-12. These sources support the test methodology; they do not establish that any strategy is profitable.

1. **MT5 bar extraction and coverage.** `copy_rates_range` returns OHLC, tick volume, spread and real volume, selected by inclusive bar-open timestamps. MetaQuotes documents UTC timestamps and recommends UTC-aware request datetimes. Available bars are limited to the terminal's chart history and its **Max. bars in chart** setting. A successful request therefore does not establish a complete requested year. Record actual first/last timestamps, bar count and missing intervals, and exclude unfinished bars. [MetaQuotes: copy_rates_range](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/python_metatrader5/mt5copyratesrange_py)

2. **Executable quotes.** `copy_ticks_range` returns bid/ask, time and flags; `COPY_TICKS_INFO` selects bid/ask changes and `COPY_TICKS_ALL` all ticks. Requests and results use UTC. The documented sample includes millisecond timestamps. Inference for this test: use ordered bid/ask quotes to model entry, exit and liquidation value, retaining the original sequence when timestamps tie. Tick requests still require an explicit coverage audit. [MetaQuotes: copy_ticks_range](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/python_metatrader5/mt5copyticksrange_py)

3. **Spread and generated-data limits.** Real-tick spreads can change inside a minute; generated ticks use the minute bar's fixed spread. Trading uses Bid and Ask, even when a chart uses Last. MT5 can generate ticks where minute history exists without tick history. Inference: a historical bar spread is an approximation, and calling a run “real ticks” is insufficient without checking data gaps and tester logs. Commission and execution slippage also require explicit test assumptions. [MetaTrader 5 Help: Real and Generated Ticks](https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/algotrading/tick_generation)

4. **Intrabar path and drawdown.** MetaQuotes explains that OHLC does not reveal the times of High and Low and that reduced testing modes can produce misleadingly attractive results. Inference: when a stop and target are both inside a bar, their actual order is unknown. Treating the stop as first is a conservative convention, not recovered history. Close-only balance checks miss floating losses; OHLC equity estimates cannot certify every intrabar breach, especially across overlapping positions and variable spreads. Report the convention and the limitation. [MetaQuotes: Testing Trading Strategies](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/runtime/testing)

5. **Selection bias.** Bailey, Borwein, Lopez de Prado and Zhu, *The Probability of Backtest Overfitting*, revised February 2015, explain how choosing configurations on historical performance can fit noise and why a holdout alone does not account for the number of trials. Inference: predeclare a small candidate set, retain every result, disclose parameter trials, and separate selection data from later evaluation. A positive recent month/year is a historical result, not evidence of assured future profitability; overlapping month/year windows are not independent confirmations. [Original author-hosted paper, especially pp. 3–7](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf)

## Prop-rule scope still unresolved

The firm, account program and evaluation phase are unspecified. The user confirmed external 4% daily / 8% total limits and their preferred internal 3% / 6% buffers. The study uses the repository's $10,000 baseline. The percentage limits are user-confirmed inputs; the detailed contractual calculation and reset rules remain unverified. Do not assign them to a named firm.

Certification needs that account's current official terms: the daily reference balance/equity, floating P&L and fee treatment, static versus trailing total loss, reset timezone and DST, news windows, overnight/weekend holding, and any minimum-day or consistency conditions. Until those are known, report “no modeled breach under the stated assumptions,” with news compliance explicitly unverified. A UTC data timestamp does not itself define the firm's reset day.
