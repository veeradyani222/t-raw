Read before trading

  • All of this is a backtest. Fills, spreads and commission are modelled; slippage beyond 0.1 pip, swaps and broker outages are not.
  • Most of the strongest strategies trade gold upward, and gold roughly doubled in these two years. If gold goes sideways, expect less.
  • The max-profit and aggressive portfolios were tuned on the same two years they are scored on. On 2015 to 2024 they break the limits; that older period is shown for reference, not as a requirement.
  • "Challenges passed / failed" starts a fresh $10,000 challenge every two weeks across the period and applies the firm's rule from each start. A mix that keeps the rule from Oct 2024 can still fail from a later start if it hits a bad stretch before building a cushion.
  • The $300 open-risk cap only applies once funded. During the challenge it is monitored, not enforced.
  • Your current live bots (CHFJPY session and gold BOS) break the 4% / 8% rule at every risk level once real spreads are included, so neither appears here.
