# FundedNext MT5: session-strategy rules and cost check

Checked 2026-09-13 against FundedNext's English first-party pages. The user's stated 4% daily / 8% total limits match **Stellar Lite**; their actual purchased model, account size, stage and add-ons have not been inspected. The research baseline remains $10,000 and internal 3% / 6% limits.

## Rules relevant to the 12-hour H1 JPY strategy

| Item | Current published rule | Implication for this research |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loss limits | Stellar Lite: 4% daily, 8% static maximum loss; balance or equity must stay above the maximum-loss floor. | On $10,000, firm daily allowance is $400 and static floor is $9,200. User's internal daily allowance is $300 and floor $9,400. Floating losses matter. [Stellar Lite rules](https://help.fundednext.com/en/articles/9094072-what-rules-do-i-need-to-follow-in-the-stellar-lite-challenge) |
| Daily accounting | Daily closed results plus open-position P&L, including commissions, fees and swaps. Reset at 00:00 server time; server is GMT+3 in DST and GMT+2 otherwise. Previous-day realized gains stop offsetting open losses after reset. | A closed-balance-only replay does not validate this rule. Reset must follow FundedNext server day, not an assumed UTC or India day. Exact seasonal change dates are not supplied by this article. [Daily-loss calculation](https://help.fundednext.com/en/articles/8019811-how-can-i-calculate-the-daily-loss-limit) |
| Automation | Current EA-specific page allows MT4/MT5 bots only for accounts **below $50,000**, with an additional EA usage fee. Accounts $50,000 and above must be fully manual in challenge and funded phases. | The $10,000 baseline is size-eligible; actual EA add-on status is unknown. A Python MT5 trading bot is automation; using Python does not avoid this policy. The broad Lite overview says EAs allowed, but its dedicated EA page provides these narrower conditions. [EA policy](https://help.fundednext.com/en/articles/8020763-is-ea-allowed-in-fundednext) |
| Concurrent exposure | Funded accounts normally have 3% maximum cumulative risk measured against **initial balance**. Risk-limit card is monitoring-only in the challenge. Every trade requires SL; missing SL is treated as 100% risk. | Separate 1%-risk pair tests cannot be added together and called a compliant portfolio. Three positions at 1% of a grown equity balance can exceed 3% of initial capital. Existing BOS exposure must count too. [Clarity Cards](https://help.fundednext.com/en/articles/15644011-what-are-the-clarity-cards-and-how-do-they-affect-my-account) |
| Risk-limit consequences | First violation: warning plus deduction of violating trade profits. Second: permanent reduction to 1% cumulative allowed risk and profit deduction. Swap and commission are excluded from this separate risk-utilization calculation. | Do not confuse the 3% concurrent-risk rule with the user's 3% daily-loss buffer. Costs still count for daily drawdown. [Risk limits](https://help.fundednext.com/en/articles/14702245-what-are-the-risk-limits-on-a-fundednext-account) |
| News | Challenge: no news profit adjustment. Funded Lite: opening or closing within 5 minutes before/after a listed directly relevant high-impact event counts only 40% of winning trade profit; losses count fully. SL/TP execution is included. Holding through news without such execution is allowed. | A mechanical 12-hour exit can enter the news window even if the original entry did not. Existing reported profits have not had this funded-phase adjustment applied. [News rules](https://help.fundednext.com/en/articles/10701447-is-news-trading-allowed-at-fundednext) |
| Holding | Overnight and weekend holding allowed in both challenge and funded Lite. Swaps apply to swap accounts, and forex triple swap is Wednesday. | Twelve-hour holding itself is permitted. Check actual rollover crossing and symbol specifications; current JPY research does not model swaps. [Holding policy](https://help.fundednext.com/en/articles/11982358-does-fundednext-allow-holding-trades-over-the-night-weekend) |

The entry-hour research labels an H1 candle by its opening hour and evaluates the signal when that candle finishes. This is not proof that `hour=0` means an execution at FundedNext midnight. Historical feed timestamps and actual FundedNext execution time must be aligned before transferring this session rule.

## Costs and what was actually checked

The parent task's read-only local MT5 account check returned **MetaQuotes-Demo / MetaQuotes Ltd / USD**, not a FundedNext server. It therefore cannot establish FundedNext bid/ask spreads or commissions. No account number or credentials are included here.

FundedNext advertises spreads starting at 0.0 pips; that is a minimum, not evidence for the session's typical spread. Its general conditions page lists Stellar Lite forex commission as $7 and says commissions are charged **per side**. Read literally, that suggests $14 round trip for the stated charge basis, so the research's $7-per-lot round-trip assumption is not verified for Lite. Confirm units and both entry/exit deal charges against the actual Lite account's specifications/ledger before replacing the model. [Symbols and conditions](https://fundednext.com/general-rules/cfds/symbols-and-conditions)

The parent task also found a differently worded Stellar Instant help page, but that is a different account model and does not settle Lite pricing. The public [Symbols page](https://fundednext.com/symbols) yielded column labels without live spread values in text extraction. Consequently, **2.5 pips remains an assumed backtest spread**, with 5 pips a stress scenario. A FundedNext bid/ask sample around the strategy's actual entry and exit times, plus its commission ledger, is still needed.

This check did not rerun backtests, change live settings, purchase add-ons, or place orders. The earlier JPY profit tables remain exploratory; they do not yet establish FundedNext payout returns or compliance under intratrade floating losses, news adjustments and actual costs.
