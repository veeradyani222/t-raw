# Breakout family: notes (in-sample 2015-2023 only)

Judge: `research/prop/harness.py`. All numbers are in-sample. Costs are the harness defaults ($14/lot RT commission on FX and metals, spread floors, slippage). Results are at `best_risk`, the highest risk where worst day stays above -3% and max DD above -6%. No out-of-sample data was read.

Files:
- `strategies.py`: all builders. The candidates are `build_de40_xetra_orb`, `build_jpy_inside_day`, `build_gold_h4_donchian` and `build_gbpjpy_prev_week`. The generic builders are `build_session_orb`, `build_pdhl`, `build_nr_break`, `build_squeeze`, `build_donchian`, `build_crabel`, `build_prev_week` and `build_combo`.
- `batches.py`, `run.py`, `results.jsonl`: every config scored (resumable, single or multi-process). `show.py` and `make_table.py` print the results.
- `causal_check.py`: truncation test. For every builder, signals built on a df cut at row k are identical to the first k signals built on the full df. 0 mismatches.
- `diag.py`: failure-pattern breakdown of trade R by side, hour, weekday, year, trend alignment, stop size and exit reason.
- `check_de40.py`, `check_stress.py`: clock sanity (volume spikes at 10:00 and 16:30 server, so the Xetra and NY opens are where expected) and spread x2/x3 cost stress.

## What was tried (about 570 unique scored runs, 9 timeframe and symbol families)
1. **Session range ORBs.** Asian range into the London break (0-9 and 0-10, entries until 14 or 15). The archived gold box 1-4. London ORB 10-11 and 10-12. NY ORB 16-17 on H1 and 16:30-17:00 on M30. A "late" day-range (1-14/15/16) into the NY break. Tested on gold, silver, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, GBPJPY, EURJPY, US30, US500, USTEC and DE40. Variants covered TP 1/1.5/2/none, stop at the far side or the midpoint, a trend filter, and break-even/trail.
2. **Previous-day high/low.** Both the breakout and the failed-break fade.
3. **NR4 / NR7 / inside-day / NR4+inside** break of yesterday's range on H1 and H4. Tested on all 23 symbols.
4. **Bollinger squeeze** breakout on H1 and H4. **Donchian** 20/30/40/55/80/120 on H4 and D1, plus a 220-bar channel on H1 (about H4-55) across all symbols.
5. **Crabel stretch ORB** with NR4 filter (re-implemented from the MQL5 code-base description "Opening Range Breakout with NR4/NR7 Contraction Filters"; no code downloaded or run).
6. **Previous-week range breakout** with an ATR stop, break-even and trail, flat on Friday.
7. **Same-symbol combos** (prev-week + inside-day + Donchian on one symbol).

## What failed and why
- **Intraday session ORBs on gold, FX and US indices lose everywhere** (about 150 runs, nearly all negative, no safe risk). Gold's Asian-range break has a gross edge near zero. The diag shows -0.003R net over 1,417 trades. Longs are -0.04R, shorts +0.03R, with no stable hour or range-size pocket. Costs (about 0.05-0.1R per trade with range-sized stops) turn it negative. The same holds on M30. The archived-style gold box 1-4 is one of the worst (-$37/mo even at 0.25%).
- **Failed-break fades** of the previous day's high/low lose too. False breaks are not a reliable reversal signal here either. Gold is just noisy intraday.
- **Late day-range into NY breakouts** lose on all 8 markets except a flat DE40.
- **US index opening-range breaks** (US30, US500, 16:30) are negative on M30 2018-2023. USTEC looks great but has only 18 months of data (2022-07 on), which makes the sample too weak. It is ignored.
- **NR7/NR4/inside-day** is flat or negative on majors, gold, silver, indices and 4 of 6 JPY crosses.
- **Combos** did not help. Merging weak signals on one symbol raised DD as fast as profit, so best_risk fell to 0.25%.
- **Tighter stops** (half range, 0.25 x ATR) raise R-expectancy but raise DD proportionally and become cost-fragile (DE40 0.25-ATR stop drops to $24/mo at 2x spread). Rejected.

## What worked (all modest, nothing near a fast challenge pass)
1. **DE40 Xetra-open 30-min range breakout** (`build_de40_xetra_orb`). It is the only robust intraday breakout found. At 0.5% risk: $48/mo, maxDD -$457, worst day -$50, 618 trades, every calendar year 2018-2023 positive. Neighbours:
   - Entry window te: 11.0 gives $9, 11.5 $33, 12.0 $47, 12.5 $48, 13.0 $44, 14.0 $18, 16.0 $14.
   - Exit 18 beats 17, 20 and 22. TP 2/3/none all positive.
   - Range 30/60/90 min all positive. No trend filter: positive but half the profit per DD. Long-only and short-only both positive.
   - H1 2015-2023 version (60-min range): positive ($14-17/mo at 0.5%). M15 (2022+) positive.
   - 2x spread: $38/mo. 3x spread: $29/mo.
   - Weakness: it does NOT transfer to other markets' opens (US indices, gold, FX are all negative). The edge is DAX-specific, consistent with the known Xetra-open auction effect. Median challenge pass is about 440-555 days, so it is too slow for an 8% phase alone.
2. **Inside-day break on USDJPY / GBPJPY** (`build_jpy_inside_day`, tp 3R). USDJPY: $31/mo at 0.75% (tp 4: $34, tp 2.5: $28, tp 2: $20), 6/9 years positive. GBPJPY: $15/mo at 0.5%, 7/9 years positive. Every tp, hold and stop neighbour is positive on both. It does not work on AUD/CAD/CHF/NZD-JPY, so this is a two-pair effect and should be treated with suspicion. 2x spread: $27 and $11.
3. **Gold H4 Donchian + daily-trend filter** (`build_gold_h4_donchian`). Positive for every n from 20 to 120 and stops of 1.5-3 ATR ($9-23/mo). The diag shows longs +0.35R and shorts -0.02R, with profit concentrated in the 2019-2020 bull run. This is mostly gold beta, so expect it to fail in a falling or ranging gold market.
4. **GBPJPY previous-week breakout** (`build_gbpjpy_prev_week`). All 5 variants positive ($4-16/mo). Gold prev-week is also positive in all 5 variants ($5-10). The other 18 symbols are mostly negative.

## Lessons
- Costs decide intraday breakouts. With range-sized stops the commission plus spread is 5-10% of R, and gold/FX session breaks have less gross edge than that.
- A daily-trend filter (yesterday's close vs daily EMA50) roughly halves DD with little loss of profit on the things that work (DE40 ORB, gold Donchian). It does not rescue things that don't work.
- The binding constraint is always max DD (-6%), not the daily limit. Breakout strategies have win rates of 40-50% and long losing streaks, so best_risk sits at 0.25-0.75% and profits stay at $15-60/month in-sample. None of these alone passes a +8% phase quickly.
- The most realistic use is a **portfolio of uncorrelated small edges**: DE40 ORB (European morning), USDJPY inside-day, gold H4 trend. The harness scores one symbol at a time, so the portfolio is untested.
- The `multiprocessing.Pool` runners must be killed when a worker dies of OOM. A hung pool silently held about 5GB on this 7.5GB machine.
