# Trend family - research notes (in-sample 2015-01 .. 2023-12 only)

Judge: `research/prop/harness.py` (FundedNext Stellar Lite $10k; SAFE = worst day > -3%,
max DD > -6%; ranking = avg_month at the highest safe risk). No 2024+ data was read.

## Files
- `strategies.py` - indicators + one shared exit engine (`_emit`) + builders:
  `build_ma_cross, build_donchian, build_supertrend, build_keltner_bo, build_bollinger_bo,
  build_macd, build_ichimoku, build_psar, build_tsmom, build_adx_di, build_htf_breakout,
  build_htf_pullback, build_donchian_volx` and the 5 candidate wrappers
  `build_gold_boll80, build_gold_boll50, build_gold_boll200_h1, build_gold_donch80, build_audjpy_boll20`.
- `run.py` (batch scorer, resumable, writes `results.jsonl`), `grid1..grid6.py` (every run below),
  `report.py` (summary).
- Causality: every builder was checked by re-building on a truncated frame and comparing
  signals bar-for-bar with the full-frame build (all identical). Day-trend filters use
  only fully closed prior broker days (`daily_trend`, shift by one day).

## Exit engine (same for all)
Initial stop `sl_mult x ATR(14)`, chandelier trail `highest-high-since-entry - 3 x ATR`
(mirrored for shorts), plus a rule exit (back through the mid-band / opposite Donchian /
indicator flips), and the opposite entry flips the position. One position, every trade stopped.

## What was tried (963 scored runs)
1. Grid 1 - 10 classic rules (EMA 20/100, Donchian 55/20, Supertrend 10/3, Keltner 20/2,
   Bollinger 20/2, MACD with zero filter, Ichimoku, PSAR, TSMOM-50, ADX/DI) on all 23 symbols x H1/H4/D1.
2. Grid 2 - one-factor neighbourhoods (Donchian lengths, stop width, trail width, no trail,
   TSMOM look-backs, event vs state re-entry, EMA pairs, long only, EMA200 / ADX filters) on
   XAUUSD H4/D1, USDJPY H4/D1, GBPUSD D1.
3. Grid 3 - gold H4 Donchian 80-150 x stop 2/3/4, cross-symbol carry of the H4 rule,
   H1 breakouts/pullbacks filtered by the closed-day trend.
4. Grid 4 - drawdown cutters on gold H4 (day-trend filter, ATR-expansion filter, exit lengths,
   trail width, ATR length) and other entry styles with the same exit.
5. Grid 5 - Bollinger breakout n 20/50/80 x k 1.5/2/2.5 x stop 2/3 on XAUUSD, AUDJPY, GBPJPY, USDJPY, US30 H4.
6. Grid 6 - cross-symbol / cross-timeframe check of the gold Bollinger rule, long-only vs
   short-only split, EMA200 filter, trail variants.

## What failed and why
- **H1 trend following is dead everywhere** (0 of ~230 H1 classic runs had a safe risk with
  positive net). Win rate ~30-35%, costs + whipsaw eat the edge; drawdowns of 40-180 R.
- **FX majors (EURUSD, GBPUSD H4, AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF), EURGBP, EURCHF, AUDCAD,
  XAGUSD, CHFJPY, EURJPY, US500, DE40** - no trend rule worked on any timeframe in-sample.
- **Buy-the-dip-in-trend (RSI2 pullback with day trend) on H1** - strongly negative on all 5
  symbols (-70 to -245 R): small targets, costs, and the day trend is too slow a filter.
- **TSMOM "state" mode** (re-enter every bar while trend is on) - more trades, worse DD.
- **ATR-expansion filter** and **EMA200 / ADX filters** - fewer trades, no better ratio.
- D1 rules on gold look good per trade but have only 25-100 trades in 9 years, and worst
  day = a full stop, so they cannot be levered; avg_month stays < $20.
- USTEC D1 numbers (PSAR $56/mo) are an artefact: 2-10 trades only (min-lot skips), ignore.

## What worked / what we learned
- **Gold (XAUUSD) H4 is the only place classic trend following is solidly positive**: every
  one of the 10 grid-1 rules was net positive on gold H4 and D1. Of the entry styles, a
  **Bollinger-band breakout with a long window (n 50-80, k 1.5-2.5)** is best and forms a
  broad plateau: 12 of 12 settings with n 50/80 are positive, $19-34/mo, return/maxDD 4.8-8.1.
  Shorts contribute too (short-only alone is positive), so it is not just gold's drift.
  Same horizon on H1 (Bollinger 200 on H1 = 50 on H4) also works ($22/mo) - good sign.
- JPY crosses (AUDJPY, GBPJPY, USDJPY) and US30 on H4 are weakly positive across most
  settings ($5-20/mo) - real but small.
- Stop width mostly changes leverage, not edge: with a 3xATR chandelier trail, a 3-4 ATR
  initial stop just sizes the trade smaller and lets the harness pick a higher risk bucket.
  Compare by return/maxDD, not by avg_month alone (risk buckets are coarse).
- **Honest ceiling: ~$20-35/month at $10k** (0.2-0.35%/month) for the best robust trend rule.
  Rolling challenge simulation: no breaches, but median time to pass both phases is
  ~900-1300 days. Trend following alone will not pass a challenge in a useful time; it may
  be useful as a low-correlation sleeve next to faster strategies.
